Why I’m here
Most tools either shout or stay silent at the wrong time. I build the opposite.

Systems that explain themselves	Every verdict comes with a reason you can act on
Contracts over cleverness	If a claim is not verified, it says so
Local first	Your machine, your tokens, your control
Fail loudly	A quiet bug is a landmine


Quiet focus. Clean solutions.
Ship the smallest thing that removes a real blocker.
